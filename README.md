# RG Wealth – Faayda Aapka
### Premium Financial Services & Stock Market Training Institute Website

A modern, responsive, and production-grade website for **RG Wealth – Faayda Aapka**, a financial services and stock market training company founded by **Rohit Garg** based in Bathinda, Punjab, India.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Key Sections

Built strictly using **HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5.3, and Vanilla JavaScript** (no React, Next.js, Vue, or Tailwind frameworks).

1. **Header & Navigation**: Sticky navbar with scroll shadow transitions, active link state, mobile menu toggle, and instant consultation CTA.
2. **Hero Section**: "Your Financial Growth, Our Commitment", financial market growth graphics, floating indicators, and verified track record (18+ years, established 2015).
3. **About RG Wealth**: Company heritage, Bathinda roots, 18+ years of expertise in Stock Market, Mutual Funds, and FNO/Commodities Trading, plus Rohit Garg's philosophy: *"There could be a bear market at some point. But what if the market doubles before that? That's why we follow a rule that we don't predict, we respond."*
4. **Our Services (6 Distinct Cards & Modals)**:
   - **Stock Market Classes**: Beginner & professional offline training.
   - **Mutual Funds**: Leading distributor in Bathinda, SIPs, ELSS tax saving, child education funds.
   - **Stock Market Services**: Portfolio Management, short-term trading, FNO & option trading.
   - **Demat Services**: Physical share dematerialization, IEPF recovery, family gift transfers.
   - **Insurance Services**: Corporate partnership with **Ideal Insurance** (Health specialization, motor, term life, corporate).
   - **Financial Planning**: Goal-based asset allocation and wealth protection.
5. **Stock Market Training Institute**: New venture launched in 2024 by Rohit Garg & Mr. Gagan Garg; over 100 students completed offline training in Bathinda with practical curriculum and inquiry form.
6. **Founder's Journey Timeline**: Step-by-step entrepreneurial milestones (Clerk beginnings -> 2007 Broking Operations -> 2010 Anand Rathi -> 2014 Axis Securities & First Venture -> 2019 RG Wealth Bathinda -> Ideal Insurance partnership -> 2024 Training Institute).
7. **Why Choose RG Wealth**: 6 factual strengths.
8. **Gallery & Company Photos**: Responsive grid with interactive JavaScript lightbox modal (keyboard and button controls).
9. **Interactive Tools**:
   - Compounding **SIP Wealth Calculator** with real-time sliders and calculations.
   - Client-side validated **Contact Consultation Form** with Indian 10-digit mobile check and loading states.
   - Client-side validated **Course Inquiry Form**.
10. **Regulatory Disclaimers**: SEBI / AMFI compliant risk disclaimer prominently displayed in the dark navy footer.

## 🚀 How to Run in VS Code or Locally

1. Open this folder in **VS Code**.
2. Right-click on `index.html` and click **"Open with Live Server"** (using the Live Server extension), or simply double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. All dependencies (Bootstrap 5.3 and Bootstrap Icons) are loaded securely via high-speed CDNs. No `npm install` or build step is required for basic local editing.
