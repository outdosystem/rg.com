Founder's Story

Rohit Garg – A Visionary Entrepreneur

Journey of Perseverance and Success

The story of RG Wealth is an inspiring testament to discipline, relentless self-improvement, and entrepreneurial courage. Rohit Garg did not inherit a broking firm; he built his expertise ground-up through every operational tier of Indian financial markets.

"There could be a bear market at some point. But what if the market doubles before that? That's why we follow a rule that we don't predict, we respond."

— Rohit Garg (Founder, RG Wealth)

This core principle anchors our investment advice and trading mentorship: reacting systematically to data and price structure instead of relying on emotional guesswork.

Phase 1
Early Career
  • Humble Beginnings: Rohit Garg began as an office clerk while pursuing his graduation through private studies.
  • 2007: First Job in Broking: Joined a stock broking firm, handling operations, back-office settlement, and customer requests.
Phase 2
Professional Growth
  • 2010: Anandrathi Group: Joined Anandrathi Group as a Relationship Manager with a starting salary of ₹10,000, cultivating wealth management acumen.
  • 2014: Axis Securities: Appointed Team Leader at Axis Securities before leaving corporate employment to chase entrepreneurial dreams.
Phase 3
Entrepreneurial Journey
  • 2014: First Venture: Founded a small independent office with a partner, taking the first leap into business.
  • 2019: Founding RG Wealth: Established RG Wealth, Bathinda's renowned broking firm, offering mutual funds, IPOs, demat accounts, equities, FNO, commodities, and portfolio management.
Phase 4
Expanding Horizons & 2024 Institute
  • Corporate Tie-up with Ideal Insurance: Partnered with corporate broker Ideal Insurance to offer health, motor, life, and commercial coverages.
  • 2024: Training Institute Launch: Partnered with Mr. Gagan Garg to establish RG Wealth Stock Market Training Institute, successfully training over 100 offline students.
