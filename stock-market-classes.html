Structured Modules

Course Modules & Highlights

Carefully structured to transition complete beginners into knowledgeable, risk-conscious market participants.

Beginner Stock Market Course
  • Introduction to Indian capital markets (NSE & BSE)
  • Understanding Demat, Trading accounts & Depository participants
  • Types of orders: Market, Limit, SL, and CNC/MIS
  • Basic reading of indices: NIFTY 50, SENSEX, Bank Nifty
Professional Trading Education
  • Advanced Candlestick patterns & Price Action setups
  • Dynamic Support & Resistance, trendlines, channels
  • Multi-timeframe analysis for swing & intraday trades
  • Risk-to-reward ratios and position sizing principles
Trading Fundamentals & Mindset
  • Overcoming greed, fear, and FOMO in live markets
  • Maintaining a structured Trading Journal
  • Strict stop-loss discipline and capital preservation
  • How to survive bear market phases and drawdown cycles
Investment Strategies
  • Long-term value investing vs. momentum trading
  • Evaluating company financials, PE ratios, debt levels
  • Dividend growth portfolios and power of compounding
  • Asset allocation across equities, mutual funds & debt
Market Analysis & Indicators
  • Volume analysis and breakout confirmations
  • Moving Averages (20, 50, 200 EMA) and RSI divergence
  • Sector rotation analysis and macroeconomic cues
  • FII & DII activity and derivative open interest (OI) data
F&O & Options Trading Education
  • Futures contracts mechanics, lot sizes, margin requirements
  • Call and Put Options: Greeks (Delta, Theta, Gamma, Vega)
  • Option buying vs. Option selling with hedged spreads
  • Straddles, Strangles, Iron Condors & capital protection rules
