Service 04

Demat Services

Paper-to-Digital Conversion & Gift Transfers.

Many families in Punjab hold ancestral paper share certificates that require dematerialization, signature verification, or transmission due to bereavement. RG Wealth provides complete hands-on operational liaison.

  • Physical Share Dematerialization: Converting paper share certificates of listed companies into electronic Demat accounts.
  • Gift Transfer Services: Facilitating legitimate off-market and on-market gift transfers to children and spouses.
  • IEPF Claims Assistance: Recovering shares and unclaimed dividends transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF).
  • Comprehensive Support: Assistance with name differences, joint holder deletion, and probate documentation.
