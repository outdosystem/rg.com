Legal & Data Ethics

Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Our steadfast commitment to digital ethics, transparent data collection, and personal information security.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version: 1.0 (Executive Office Template)

1. Executive Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how the executive personal branding website of Rohit Garg ("we", "our", or "the Office") collects, uses, protects, and handles personal data submitted by visitors, event organizers, clients, and readers.

We adhere to strict principles of data minimization and confidentiality. We only collect information strictly necessary to facilitate speaking requests, advisory dialogues, book orders, and professional collaborations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you directly provide through our online forms:

  • Contact Information: Full name, corporate email address, telephone or WhatsApp contact number.
  • Professional Details: Company or institutional affiliation, designation, website URL.
  • Inquiry Details: Preferred speaking session themes, tentative dates, attendee estimates, and custom messages.
  • Publication Inquiries: Inscription preferences and shipping destination for book orders.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is used solely for the following legitimate professional purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries submitted via our contact, session booking, and review forms.
  • Coordinating logistical schedules and contracts for keynote speaking engagements.
  • Fulfilling requests for copies of The Principles of Trust.
  • Publishing authorized client feedback with appropriate consent.

Zero Commercial Resale: Under no circumstances do we sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third-party marketing brokers or advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Google Services

This website integrates authorized client reviews via official Google Places API endpoints and links to external platforms (such as Google Maps, LinkedIn, and YouTube). When interacting with external profiles, your engagement is governed by their respective privacy terms and cookie policies.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL/TLS transmission protocols, and restricted administrative access to safeguard all transmitted communications. Data is retained only for the duration required to service your professional engagement, after which it is archived securely or deleted upon request.

6. Your Rights

You retain full rights to:

  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold regarding your inquiries.
  • Request rectification of erroneous information.
  • Request complete deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") from our communications registry.

7. Contact the Data Governance Desk

For any privacy questions or requests to inspect or remove personal records, please reach out directly:
